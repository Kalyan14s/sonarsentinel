## A3 — synthetic ghost net

> **SYNTHETIC.** The ghost net is rendered by the SonarSentinel generator (`ml/synth/ghost_net_generator.py`, v1.0.0, fixed seed) on a real, object-free seabed image. The coordinates are a **fictional** straight track off Chennai harbour, not a real survey. Always present it as synthetic.

- **Image:** 640 × 640 px at 0.10 m/px; the net is about 8 × 4 m, 1 m high, on the port-side seabed (clear of the dark water-column band) with a far-range shadow. Geometry is idealised: the navigation and the ground truth both assume nadir at the image centre (`port_stbd` layout), while the water-column band of this background sits right of centre, so positions are self-consistent but not a physical survey.
- **Navigation:** `harbour_synthetic.csv` in the template columns (`ping`, `time_utc`, `lat`, `lon`, `heading_deg`, `slant_range_m`, `altitude_m`, `sensor_depth_m`), one row every 20 image rows; the reader interpolates the rest.
- **Ground truth:** `harbour_synthetic.truth.json` has the net's pixel box, approximate position, size and every generator parameter.
- **Background licence:** Mine SSS dataset (D2), **CC BY 4.0** — see [ml/datasets/LICENSES.md](../ml/datasets/LICENSES.md). Attribution must stay with the image.

Regenerate (needs the converted Mine SSS images in `data/interim/mine_sss`):

```bash
python scripts/make_demo_assets.py --out demo
sonarsentinel detect demo/harbour_synthetic.png --nav demo/harbour_synthetic.csv --out results/
```

Or upload both files on the Upload screen.

**Measured on 2026-09-14** (`sonarsentinel detect` with the default models: `yolo11s-seg-sonar-real@0.1.0` via ONNX Runtime, PatchCore, FP filter, isotonic calibrator): 19 detections in 7 s. The only detection within 5 m of the net is a `cylinder` at 15.2% confidence (`hidden` tier), 2.6 m from the true position. The other 18 are seabed ripples and water-column edges 15–40 m away, all low confidence. The CPU-baseline detector was trained without ghost nets (EXP-20260913-baseline), so the ghost-net story in the demo needs the GPU-trained detector (carried ST-051). Until then, present this asset as "pipeline and geotagging on a synthetic target", not as a ghost-net detection.
