## 1. Hypothesis
Adding synthetic ghost-net tiles (generator v1, ST-016) to the real training data teaches the detector the `ghost_net` class, lifting ghost-net recall on the synthetic holdout (unseen site-2017 backgrounds) from 0.00 to at least 0.60 (risk R1 trigger), without lowering real `cylinder` validation mAP@50 beyond the baseline's CI.

## 2. Baseline
[EXP-20260913-baseline](EXP-20260913-baseline.md): val mAP@50 box 0.283 (95% CI 0.174–0.456), mask 0.280; ghost-net holdout recall 0.00.

## 3. What changed
Training data only: 250 synthetic ghost-net tiles added (`prepare_yolo.py --variant real_synth`). Model, schedule, augmentations and seed are identical to the baseline.

## 6. Observations
- A fair ablation must keep batch 8; halving the batch to fit in memory would change the schedule and make the comparison with EXP-20260913-baseline unfair unless the baseline is rerun the same way.
- The risk R1 check (ghost-net recall < 0.60 → ST-055) can't be made until this run finishes. The baseline's 0.00 says only that a model without ghost-net data finds no ghost nets.
